What we do

End-to-end IT services, from the first migration plan to day-two operations, with security and compliance evidence built in from the start.

Cloud migration
Move existing applications and infrastructure to the cloud with a plan your security team has already signed off on.
Kubernetes platform engineering
Production clusters on EKS, GKE and AKS with service mesh, workload identity and policy applied consistently across all three.
AI application development
We design and build generative AI applications: retrieval-augmented search over your own documents, AI agents, document analysis, and compliance checks for AI itself.
Multi-cloud AI deployment
We deploy and operate those applications on AWS, Google Cloud and Azure, under the same controls as the rest of your estate, so you can run each workload where it fits best.
DevSecOps and automation
Infrastructure as code and delivery pipelines that test, scan and record every change before it reaches production.
Security and compliance engineering
Turn frameworks such as SOC 2 and NIST into working controls: MFA, encryption in transit, logging, web application firewalls.
Managed operations
We run your infrastructure day to day: monitoring, patching, incident response and cost reviews.
Software engineering and QA
Requirements analysis, system design, development, integration and quality assurance for complex applications.
Staffing and procurement
Direct hires, contract engineers and fixed-price teams, plus sourcing of the IT equipment and supplies a project needs.
